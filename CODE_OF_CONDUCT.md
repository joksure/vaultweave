# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what is best
for the project and its users. The following are not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
trolling, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

To report a problem, use the private **Security → Report a vulnerability** form on this repository or contact the
maintainers listed in the repository. Reports are handled confidentially.
